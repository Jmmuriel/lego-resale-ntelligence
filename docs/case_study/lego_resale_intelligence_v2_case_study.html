01 · The Problem
From analyzer to operating system
V1 proved the signal

A one-off listing analyzer was useful. Collectors need a repeatable market workflow.

V1 could turn a marketplace URL into a buying signal. V2 expands the job: track sets, portfolio exposure, price history, watchlists, briefings and data confidence.

MARKETCollectors need set trends and context, not only one listing at a time.
WATCHInteresting opportunities should be tracked, filtered and revisited.
PROOFSeed data must not be sold as verified live market evidence.
DEMOA portfolio project needs public links, tests and a clear story.

10 · Honest Positioning
What to say and avoid
Portfolio framing

The case study is stronger because it admits what is still seed data.

Say this

  • Seed-backed LEGO resale intelligence prototype.
  • Production-style FastAPI and Next.js architecture.
  • Data-quality gates and evidence tracking.
  • Public demo deployed on Railway and Vercel.

Avoid this

  • Fully verified live market pricing platform.
  • All 50 sets are proven real market data.
  • Price history comes from live BrickLink data.
  • Commercial resale advice without source caveats.
