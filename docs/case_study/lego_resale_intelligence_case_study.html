01 · The Problem
Marketplace noise
Signal vs noise

Marketplace listings are noisy. Good opportunities are easy to miss.

Retired LEGO sets can be valuable, but listings are inconsistent: incomplete titles, unclear condition, missing set IDs, unstable URLs and prices that are hard to compare.

SET IDOften missing, hidden, translated or mixed with marketplace noise.
PRICEAsking price is easy to see; real net margin is not.
RISKIncomplete boxes, unclear condition and missing parts change the deal.
SCOREA buyer needs a fast way to decide whether to inspect or skip.
02 · Product Promise
One URL → one score
Product flow

One URL. One score.

The app turns a real listing URL into a structured buying signal: fair price, net margin, risk flags, opportunity score and local archive.
